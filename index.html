1. Management, work permits & security

7 topics
Question / issue

Residents reported approved contractors being refused access during normal hours while a large furniture delivery and installation was apparently allowed at around 1 a.m.

Suggested solution

Residents suggested asking security for an explanation, escalating repeated inconsistencies to Dubai South Community Management (DSCM), and keeping evidence such as permit approvals and security responses. One escalation suggestion was Dubai Land Department if management would not assist.

Status: Recurring issue; escalation route suggested, no final policy clarification recorded
Question / issue

Residents asked who within Dubai South / DSCM has authority to resolve permit problems.

Suggested solution

Suggested route: Dubai South administration / DSCM first; if unresolved, escalate formally. The community email shared in the chat was thepulsevillas@dscm.ae. Residents also recommended documenting the application, rejection reason and contractor paperwork.

Status: Practical escalation path shared by residents
Question / issue

Some residents found their unit registration disappeared or the DS App asked them to register again before permits could be raised.

Suggested solution

One resident advised that tenants may need to renew the app registration after annual Ejari renewal. For OTP/app failures, residents were directed back to Dubai South / maintenance contacts.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

A resident said a contractor permit kept being declined because the provider lacked third-party liability insurance or an undated AED 2,000 cheque.

Suggested solution

Advice shared: if the provider has the required liability insurance, a cheque may not be needed; if not, the resident can provide the undated cheque and retrieve it after the work is completed.

Status: Resident-reported workaround; verify current policy before relying on it
Question / issue

Residents repeatedly asked whether app-booked cleaners, AC cleaners or handymen need a work permit.

Suggested solution

The chat contains conflicting experience: some light visits entered without a permit, while others said a permit was still needed but was approved quickly. Another useful point: Justlife may assign different third parties per task, so the final contractor documents/trade licence may only become available after team assignment.

Note: Treat permit requirements as job-specific and confirm before the team arrives; security has denied entry where a permit was missing.
Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents reported repeated doorbell ringing, scooters/bikes in roads, children entering private garages and a lack of timely security response.

Suggested solution

Suggested approach: report to security; provide villa numbers if known; request CCTV review; have an incident report logged and sent to DSCM; and ask management to contact parents / issue formal notices. The owners committee said it could escalate on residents' behalf.

Status: Incident-report/CCTV escalation was actively used
Question / issue

Residents questioned whether side access points were helping security and whether non-residents were entering too easily.

Suggested solution

A proposal raised was to close unnecessary side gates and funnel non-resident access through the main gate, potentially using controlled/electric barriers. This was discussed as a committee proposal, not a confirmed implementation.

Status: Proposal / management decision required

2. Water supply, pumps & pressure

7 topics
Question / issue

New residents reported DEWA being active but no water reaching the villa.

Suggested solution

Residents suggested checking the water tap/valve under the villa-number board, then checking the pump room for a closed valve or switched-off pump.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

A recurring community complaint is pressure rising and dropping, sometimes returning only briefly after maintenance visits.

Suggested solution

First-line suggestion: ask Duserve to check / refill air pressure in the pump system. Several residents said the original blue expansion tank is a common weak point. Replacing the blue expansion tank alone reportedly solved fluctuations in some villas.

Status: Common resident-tested fix
Question / issue

Residents reported the pump running non-stop and pressure staying high.

Suggested solution

Immediate advice was to switch the pump off to avoid damage, accepting that water supply may stop until it is switched back on. Then call Duserve / a pump technician. One case resulted in a recommendation to replace the blue tank and one pump.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Some residents had repeated pump failures or complete water cut-off and asked whether replacing the pumps is worthwhile.

Suggested solution

Reported options included buying replacement pumps directly and having Duserve install them. One 2026 example: two DAB Jetinox 132 M pumps were around AED 750 each + VAT, with Duserve installation around AED 315–400. Another resident reported a 'silent' replacement around AED 800.

Note: These are historical resident prices, not quotes.
Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents wanted a longer-term alternative to the original system that needs air top-ups, is noisy and gives variable pressure.

Suggested solution

A resident-described upgrade used one active and one backup pump with no blue air cylinder, producing stronger, more consistent pressure and much less noise. Reported installed prices varied widely (roughly AED 1,500–6,000 depending on specification/provider); one Transguard-based quote was about AED 4,500. A normal work permit describing 'pump replacement' was reported as sufficient.

Status: Strong positive resident feedback; specification varies by supplier
Question / issue

Residents saw unexplained DEWA consumption / apparent overflow.

Suggested solution

Suggested check: inspect the tank float valve even if overflow is not visible, because an overflow pipe can discharge directly to sewage. A damaged float valve was reported as the cause in at least one villa.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents posted water leaking around the parking/tank area.

Suggested solution

The float mechanism was suggested as a likely cause; Duserve was repeatedly recommended for inspection.

Status: Resident advice / experience

3. Air conditioning & HVAC

6 topics
Question / issue

A resident found 25°C too cold when the AC was on, but uncomfortable when it was off.

Suggested solution

Suggested setting: Eco mode around 24–25°C, or normal mode with the lowest fan speed. The resident offering the tip said this improved the problem substantially, though not completely.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents reported a unit still cooling poorly and smelling dusty even after coil cleaning.

Suggested solution

Duct cleaning was suggested as the next diagnostic step. Residents also recommended getting an HVAC technician to check the system rather than repeatedly cleaning the same components.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents reported bedroom AC controls going blank despite resetting breakers and roof equipment.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included checking/replacing a small fuse and contacting Duserve/maintenance. A local parts shop was later shared for a low-cost replacement part in a similar 'panel works but no air/fan' fault.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

A resident asked what to do when cleaning companies said the blower motor needed replacement.

Suggested solution

Advice was to have Duserve or an HVAC technician assess whether the motor can be serviced rather than automatically accepting a full replacement.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents saw very wide pricing and asked what a proper duct clean should include.

Suggested solution

Reported examples ranged from about AED 250 per unit to AED 2,299 for a five-unit package that included filters, ducts, blowers, drain trays, outdoor units and re-gassing. One resident specifically advised agreeing the scope and price upfront and checking that mechanical brush cleaning and approved disinfectant are included.

Note: Prices and scope are resident reports and should be re-quoted.
Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents asked who could install smart thermostats correctly.

Suggested solution

A recommended installer contact was shared (Bilal – Just Care / Google Thermostat). Residents said the installer could handle multiple thermostat brands including Ecobee.

Status: Resident advice / experience

4. Drains, smells & environmental odour

4 topics
Question / issue

Residents repeatedly reported a strong sewage / chemical-like odour, especially at night and in the early morning.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included coordinated complaints to Dubai South, Dubai Municipality and the relevant sewage-treatment/environmental authorities. Residents said previous bulk complaints had reduced the problem. In September, residents specifically urged calls to Dubai Municipality on 800 900; the chat then reported environmental monitoring equipment being deployed.

Status: Major recurring issue; official environmental attention reported in September 2026
Question / issue

Residents debated whether the source was the nearby sewage treatment plant, treated sewage irrigation water or something within the community.

Suggested solution

No single cause was verified in the chat. The guide therefore records these as resident hypotheses only. The practical consensus was to log official complaints and measurements rather than rely on speculation.

Status: Cause not established in the source chat
Question / issue

A resident reported a smell clearly coming from indoor drains rather than outside, despite repeated Duserve visits.

Suggested solution

A resident with the same problem said an infrequently used maid's-room toilet was allowing odour to return; flushing it roughly weekly prevented recurrence. This is a simple first check before bringing in an external plumber.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

A resident asked where the extraction-fan switch is located.

Suggested solution

The answer shared was: in the kitchen.

Status: Resident advice / experience

5. Noise & soundproofing

4 topics
Question / issue

Residents described hearing footsteps, doors and neighbouring activity through the shared structure.

Suggested solution

A resident said a gap + stud wall filled with rock wool and two overlapping/taped gypsum-board layers materially helped in the master bedroom and living/dining area. The staircase remained a weak point. A quote for a stairwell solution using mass-loaded vinyl and a false wall was around AED 16,000.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents asked whether wall-mounted acoustic panels would solve sound coming through from next door.

Suggested solution

The chat cautioned that products aimed at reverberation/echo may improve the room acoustics for the neighbour-facing wall but may not stop structure-borne/transmitted sound. Two contractors reportedly told one resident not to spend money on this approach for that problem.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents said road noise appeared to be entering through poor-quality frames and glazing.

Suggested solution

Contractors reportedly recommended replacing the frame and using soundproof glass, with matching exterior colour. Residents said the modification had been rejected by Dubai South because it changes the exterior. The community may need a coordinated approval route if pursuing this.

Status: Technical option identified; external modification approval unresolved
Question / issue

A resident proposed Deciboard as a possible staircase solution.

Suggested solution

It was shared as a product worth sourcing locally, but no completed installation / outcome was recorded in the chat.

Status: Idea only / not validated in the source

8. Pests, pets & animal-related issues

5 topics
Question / issue

Residents reported more mosquitoes, flies and insects entering villas.

Suggested solution

Door seals were said to reduce ingress but not eliminate it because insects also enter when doors are opened. Residents combined sealing with pest control.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents who had not previously had ants reported sudden heavy infestations.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included checking the garden for ant nests/hills and using professional pest control. One resident reported no further ants after treatment.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents in multiple phases reported possible redback spiders.

Suggested solution

Practical advice included keeping garden areas uncluttered, allowing sunlight into dark nesting areas and keeping furniture roughly 20 cm away from walls. Management pest control attended at least one property, and residents suggested requesting treatment across the wider community / municipality where multiple sightings occur.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

A dog left outside barked for a prolonged period and disturbed neighbours.

Suggested solution

Suggested escalation: speak to the neighbour first; if unresolved, contact security and then formal authorities/police if necessary. In the documented case, the owner explained an emergency and the immediate conflict was resolved through communication.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents raised two animal-welfare concerns: a cat caught in rat-glue adhesive and another cat reportedly injured by pellets.

Suggested solution

Suggestions were to place pest-control traps where pets/strays cannot access them and to report suspected deliberate animal cruelty / unsafe weapon use to the appropriate authorities and security. Residents also asked for CCTV review.

Status: Resident advice / experience

9. Roads, parking, scooters & community safety

4 topics
Question / issue

Residents repeatedly raised speeding and vehicles using one-way streets incorrectly.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included clearer directional road markings, stronger security reporting and one-way / unidirectional speed-control measures. The owners committee said unidirectional speed bumps had been requested but refused at that stage, and alternative safety measures were being sought.

Status: Safety concern remains; some proposed measures not approved
Question / issue

Residents reported a recurring group riding in the road, blocking vehicles, ringing doorbells and entering private areas.

Suggested solution

Suggested response: identify villas where possible, notify security, use CCTV, log incidents to DSCM and ask management to contact parents. Residents also called for more suitable recreation space so children have safer places to play.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents asked owners to move cars parked in another villa's space or obstructively where other bays were available.

Suggested solution

Community norm suggested: use unassigned spaces where available and ask permission before using a neighbour's space.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Some residents argued that nuisance behaviour partly reflects a lack of age-appropriate community space beyond toddler-oriented parks.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included football, basketball/tennis and better open recreation areas, and reconsidering low-use spaces such as the lagoon/pond area for more useful amenities.

Status: Community improvement proposal

11. Community budget, events & service-charge ideas

4 topics
Question / issue

With a limited events budget, residents debated multiple smaller festive events versus one larger community day.

Suggested solution

Ideas included family/kids activities, lawn sports and bouncy castles, with a larger event held in cooler months. Some preferred multiple culturally inclusive events such as Ramadan/Eid, Diwali, Holi, Christmas and Easter.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents said catering can consume a large share of event budgets.

Suggested solution

Suggested model: invite food stalls / food trucks to sell directly, potentially charging vendors for space/power rather than using community funds to provide all food.

Status: Resident advice / experience
Question / issue

Residents questioned whether service-charge money should fund social events or facilities available to non-residents.

Suggested solution

Suggestions included prioritising internal upkeep, landscaping, benches and signage; charging for events; making facilities resident-focused; or operating some amenities separately on usage fees.

Status: Resident views / proposals only
Question / issue

Residents challenged the idea that an approved budget must be spent or lost.

Suggested solution

The committee response in the chat was that approved RERA/Mollak budgets may be difficult to repurpose once approved, but management could be asked whether unused funds can roll into the following year or be redirected if community consensus supports it.

Status: Needs formal management / RERA-Mollak confirmation

12. Deliveries, lost property & neighbour-to-neighbour help

0 topics

A significant share of the group is practical mutual help. The recurring patterns are useful enough to preserve as community norms:

• Misdelivered Amazon/Noon/water/flower packages: post the intended name/villa (without sharing unnecessary personal data) and arrange collection.
• Lost sports items (balls, golf clubs, bags): check the park/court first, then security/lost property, then post in the group.
• Borrowing short-use items: residents regularly lend jumper cables, sports equipment, burners, office chairs and baby items.
• Moving / cleaning emergencies: residents often recommend app-based services such as Justlife when available, but access/permit requirements should be checked first.
• Unwanted bulky items: 800JUNK was suggested for collection; residents also offer reusable items to neighbours before disposal.

15. Open / unresolved community actions

0 topics

The following topics recur in the chat without a clearly documented permanent resolution:

Appendix: suggested community posting format

To make future WhatsApp advice easier to add to this guide, residents could use a simple format:

• ISSUE: short description
• PHASE / AREA: only if relevant
• WHAT I TRIED: steps already taken
• SOLUTION THAT WORKED: if resolved
• PROVIDER: business name + business number (avoid publishing personal resident numbers)
• COST / DATE: optional, so others know how old the price is
• PERMIT NEEDED?: yes / no / not sure
• STATUS: resolved / temporary fix / still open

Important: For safety, health, legal, emergency, utility or regulatory matters, always check current official guidance. WhatsApp advice can be useful, but it can also be incomplete or outdated.

Mobile signal coverage
Coordinate e&/Etisalat and du fault reports; ask DSCM/Dubai South to support a formal coverage/cell-site request. — Residents / management / operators
Foul outdoor odour
Continue coordinated official complaints and request measurement/source confirmation rather than relying on hypotheses. — Residents / Municipality / environmental authorities
Road safety
Revisit one-way enforcement, road markings and physical speed-control alternatives after the earlier speed-bump proposal was refused. — Owners committee / management
Side-gate access
Clarify whether non-essential side gates can be closed or access-controlled so non-residents use the main gate. — Owners committee / security / management
Soundproofing external modifications
Seek a consistent approval standard for visually matched frame/glazing upgrades that reduce road noise. — Owners / DSCM
Water pump design
Share a standard technical note comparing original system maintenance, blue-tank replacement and full constant-pressure upgrades. — Owners committee / maintenance
Pool / splash facility maintenance
Track cooler/heater, cleanliness and broken splash-park components and publish closure/reopening updates. — Facility operator / management
Resident-only facility benefits
Evaluate resident ID/card/promo code or differentiated pricing before the next budget cycle. — Management / owners committee
Community event funding
Confirm formally whether unused event budget can roll over or be repurposed under RERA/Mollak-approved budgets. — Owners committee / DSCM
Community amenities
Prioritise a resident wish-list: benches, signage, improved internal landscaping and more age-appropriate sports/play space. — Owners / management
